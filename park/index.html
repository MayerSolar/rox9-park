The idea

Return on Xperience. A place designed around the moments people actually remember.

ROX Park brings together curated retail, chef-driven dining, culture and lifestyle, alongside living and workspace. A walkable, design-led neighborhood built to be lived in, not just visited.

Retail

Curated brands and flagship stores.

A design-led village of storefronts, from local makers to international names, laid out to be walked and lingered in.

Dining

Chef-driven kitchens and open-air terraces.

Restaurants, cafés and late-night spots spilling onto shaded terraces, built around the moments people gather for.

Padel, culture, living and workspace.
